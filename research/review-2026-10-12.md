## Decisions I made for you (full list D1–D20 in `build-plan.md`)

| Decision | Default | Change if… |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | Parakeet v3 via FluidAudio, using Droppy's model and dictionary | — |
| "Me" | Whoever matches your enrolled voice. Without enrollment, the main voice on the mic. | — |
| Voiceprint auto-name | Score ≥0.75 names automatically, 0.60–0.75 asks "Is this X?" | The thresholds come from synthetic voices, so expect to tune them on your first ~5 real meetings |
| Renaming a speaker | Creates a person, so the name sticks. "This is…" links to a known person. "Rename in this meeting only" is the alternative. | — |
| Echo removal | On, but only runs when bleed is detected | It hurts your own voice in real recordings |
| Calendar prompt | **Off** until you turn it on, because turning it on is what asks for access | — |
| Auto-start on events | Off | — |
| Mic-in-use prompt, auto-stop, consent line | On | Auto-stop only arms after a call app was seen using the mic, so in-person recordings never auto-stop |
| Typed notes | In the shelf widget; the notch is a setting | — |
| Templates | General, 1:1, Standup, Sales, Interview, Custom | — |
| Audio retention | Forever. Expiry counts from when a meeting was processed, never from the recording date, and only deletes audio. | — |
| Library edits | The app's database is the source of truth. Drive's notes.md and transcript.md are re-rendered on every edit. | **Hand edits made in Drive are overwritten.** Edit in the app. |
| Attendee names in vocabulary | Whole names, stricter match (0.8) | Unusual names get corrected less often |

## Known limits and risks

- **Echo and voiceprint numbers are from synthetic audio.** Real rooms, speakers and voices will differ.
- **Speaker separation can merge similar voices:** two synthetic voices on one track became one speaker.
- **Echo removal on bleed meetings:**
  - It takes about 5 minutes per hour of audio.
  - On those meetings, the mic part of the playback audio is 16 kHz.
- **Accessibility names are untested:** Zoom, Teams and Chrome aren't installed, so names may simply not come through.
- **HUD prompts are unverified:** the calendar, call, auto-stop and countdown prompts use HUD cards with buttons, and whether those buttons are clickable in real Droppy is unknown.
- **One rare Core Audio crash path remains:** a device switch in the exact instant a recording starts. The fix would be a small Objective-C shim, which I didn't add.
- **Distribution:** the pipeline depends on this repo's Python venv, `claude` at `~/.local/bin`, and your Drive account path. It's fine for your own Mac, but not shareable or Store-publishable without porting the pipeline to a bundled Swift helper (see the stack question below).
- **Rebuilding LocalVQE needs Homebrew.** Running it doesn't: the binary has no Homebrew dependencies at runtime.
- **FluidAudio duplicate:** `~/Library/Application Support/FluidAudio/Models/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3` (461 MB) is now unused, because the pipeline reads Droppy's copy. I'll delete it after your first real recording confirms that path works.
